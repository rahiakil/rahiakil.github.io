## 3. Implicit rubric (what members actually used)

### Pricing (weight 18)
- Max **$15** volunteer lunch after rejecting India Belly at $20.  
  Evidence T-UTTORON-LUNCH: “Need something within max 15 USD”
- Tea at $3.50/person rejected for community tea makers.
- PayPal fees and non-member markup checked on every public SKU.

### Reliability (weight 16)
- PAC lunch hard window **12:20 max**.
- Sharodotsav: Nola dinner late; guests queued at Kha-Bar; refunds planned.  
  Evidence: “not be able to open dinner on time”

### Quality (weight 14)
- Cafe Bollywood Boisakhi salty biryani / mixed feedback blocked one XL path, later reopened for dual-vendor fairness.
- Mayuri lunch praised: “thanks for excellent lunch box”

### Menu variety (weight 12)
- Anjan Dutta / Bhoomi: **two vendors**, second non-Mughlai option.
- Kali: khichuri vs regular veg/non-veg poll close; kept both; kids pizza added.

### Vendor rotation (weight 12)
- Explicit annual distribution (~2 events) for Cafe Bollywood.
- Kha-Bar Kali decline letter: menu variety, vendor rotation, pricing, event scale.

### Event scale (weight 10)
- Stall value questioned at 150–200 if sales unclear.
- Dual vendors when crossing ~700.

### Dietary / logistics / relationship
- Veg vs non-veg counts always tracked; tray multiples of 30; buffer vs waste debate Oct 7; warm partners still rotated.
