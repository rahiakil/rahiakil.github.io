## 3. Implicit rubric (what members actually used)

### 3.1 Pricing (weight 18) — decisive often

- Ops lead capped volunteer lunch at **max $15** after rejecting $20.  
  Evidence T-UTTORON-LUNCH: “Need something within max 15 USD”
- Tea at $3.50/person rejected in favor of community-owned makers.  
  Evidence: “Tea is very expensive, rather go back to storage”
- Every public SKU checked for **PayPal fees and non-member markup**.  
  Evidence T-SKU-PUJO: “PayPal taxes included and markup added”
- Kali SKUs landed near veg $15–17, non-veg $19–21 with misti add-on.  
  Evidence T-KALI-PRICE: “Veg 15 / Non veg 19” then misti rounding discussion

### 3.2 Reliability (weight 16) — high impact when failed

- Hard arrival window for PAC lunch: **12:20 max**.  
  Evidence: “no room for any delay”
- Festival evening: dinner late; guests queued at another stall; refunds planned.  
  Evidence T-LATE-DINNER: “not be able to open dinner on time”

### 3.3 Quality (weight 14)

- Prior salty biryani / mixed feedback blocked one XL award path.  
  Evidence T-BOISAKHI-CB: “biriyani was salty as well (mixed feedback)”
- Positive: “thanks for excellent lunch box”; survey-side “food was all good”

### 3.4 Menu variety (weight 12)

- For 700-seat night: **two vendors**, second non-Mughlai option.  
  Evidence T-DUAL-700: “option for 2 vendors” / “non mughlai - chinese dish”
- Kali: khichuri vs regular veg/non-veg poll was close; kept both.  
  Evidence: “People were split between khichuri and regular”
- Kids pizza gap noted after artist concert; added for Kali.  
  Evidence T-KIDS-PIZZA: “Pizza for kids”

### 3.5 Vendor rotation / fairness (weight 12)

- Explicit annual distribution goal: equal representation (~2 events).  
  Evidence: “equal representation in the annual distribution”
- Decline letter lists four pillars: **menu variety, vendor rotation, pricing, event scale**.  
  Evidence T-DECLINE-LETTER (paraphrase of committee message)

### 3.6 Event scale fit (weight 10)

- Stall value questioned at 150–200 if sales unclear.  
  Evidence: “150-200 people, then asking someone for stall makes sense??”
- Dual vendors justified when crossing ~700.  
  Evidence: “we will cross 700 people”

### 3.7 Dietary / veg–non-veg (weight 8)

- Orders always split veg vs non-veg headcounts.  
  Evidence: “5 non veg 1 veg”
- Sunday vegetarian volume “very small”; tray multiples of 30 for khichuri/labra.

### 3.8 Logistics / flexibility (weight 6)

- Last-minute dinner +2 accommodated and thanked.  
- Buffer vs waste debate was the sharpest internal conflict (Oct 7).  
  Evidence: “what is our risk appetite”

### 3.9 Relationship (weight 4)

- Warm partners still declined when rotation required; messaging standardized against “emotional pressure.”
