# Yearly rubric review checklist

Use once per year (suggested: January) before the new event calendar locks.

1. Re-read `analysis/gaps.csv` and mark which gaps still recurred.
2. Open the Distribution Simulator with the past year’s ledger. Any vendor over 35% of major slots?
3. Recount decisive moments: did pricing, reliability, or rotation dominate? Adjust `config.yaml` weights so they still sum to 100.
4. Confirm lunch-box and festival SKU bands against actual invoices.
5. Decide publication flags for vendor trade names.
6. Refresh must-have gates (add allergen labeling if surveys demand it).
7. Archive the year’s `vendor_ledger.csv` snapshot under `analysis/archive/YYYY/`.
8. Resolve open questions in `analysis/report.md` section 8 with a short committee note.
