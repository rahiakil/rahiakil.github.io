Who we are

We are a volunteer food team supporting a Seattle-area cultural community calendar: festival weekends, artist nights, hike days, and smaller volunteer meals. Needs include vegetarian and non-vegetarian lines, festival trays, snacks, sweets, and reliable timing at rented venues.

Vendor names on this public site are anonymized (Vendor 1–9) unless the committee flips a publication flag in config.

Calendar pattern

  • Small volunteer lunches (under 100)
  • Medium community evenings (100–300)
  • Large festival dinners (300–600)
  • Extra-large artist or pujo weekends (600+)

At XL scale we often run two evening options so guests have choice and partners share opportunity.

Vendor philosophy

Quality and warmth matter. So does fairness. A partner we love may still sit out a given dinner when rotation, pricing, or scale say another mix serves the community better. When we decline, we say so courteously and keep the door open.
