Dietary & religious guide

Plain explanations for partners new to our community calendar. Chat evidence was strongest on veg/non-veg separation and festival dishes; several stricter certifications were rarely discussed and are marked as thin evidence.

Observed as standard

  • Vegetarian vs non-vegetarian: Always labeled; counts planned separately; trays should not blur the line.
  • No beef or pork: Community cultural default for public menus.
  • Festival trays: Items such as khichuri, labra, polau, fish curries, misti appear on major weekends.
  • Sweets: Often a small add-on SKU; shelf life and packaging matter for pre-order nights.
  • Kids options: Raised after a large artist night; now planned explicitly when families dominate.

Ask us when relevant (thin in 2026 chat)

  • Jain or no onion/garlic (satvik) menus
  • Vegan, gluten-free, nut-free lines
  • Formal halal certification
  • Documented kitchen allergen protocols

If your kitchen already supports these, say so in the quote. We will confirm per event rather than assume.

Guide derived from anonymized 2026 food-team discussions.
