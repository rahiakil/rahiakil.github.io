What we need from you

Thank you for considering a partnership with our community. This one-page brief explains how we decide, what to put in a quote, and how we follow up after the event.

How we decide

We look at menu variety, vendor rotation across the year, pricing, and event scale, plus food quality and on-time reliability. Being a valued partner does not guarantee every date. Sometimes scale or fairness means we choose a different mix. We will tell you that clearly and kindly.

Dietary & religious checklist

  • Separate, clearly labeled vegetarian and non-vegetarian items
  • No beef or pork on community menus
  • Tell us if onion/garlic-free or fasting-friendly dishes are available when we ask for a festival menu
  • Flag common allergens when you can (nuts, dairy, gluten)
  • For tray service: state portions per tray and how veg trays are kept distinct
  • Kids-friendly option when the event is family-heavy (we will say so in the brief)

Quote template (please include)

  • Per-head or per-tray price, delivery included or called out
  • Deposit amount and due date
  • Cancellation / reduction window
  • Headcount tolerance (e.g. ±10% until 48 hours prior)
  • Setup or delivery arrival window (exact clock time for venue events)
  • Backup plan if delayed
  • Invoice email: treasurer inbox we will provide

Observed planning bands (2026): volunteer lunch often capped near $15/plate; festival dinner SKUs commonly landed near $15–21 depending on veg/non-veg and sweets add-ons.

After the event

We may send a short feedback form covering taste, portion, punctuality, labeling, and communication. Your replies help us keep the relationship strong for future dates.

  1. Taste and freshness (1–5)
  2. Portions matched the plan (yes / partial / no)
  3. Arrived by agreed window (yes / no + minutes)
  4. Veg / non-veg labeling clear (yes / no)
  5. Would we invite you again for this event type? (yes / with changes / no)
